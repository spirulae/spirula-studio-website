# Site is under development and must stay out of search results.
#
# The mechanism that actually does that is the noindex meta tag in index.html,
# NOT this file. Crawling is deliberately left open: a crawler that is blocked
# here never fetches the page, so it never sees the noindex, and the bare URL
# can still end up listed. Blocking and de-indexing are opposites — pick one.
#
# To go public: drop the noindex meta from index.html, restore sitemap.xml,
# and add the Sitemap line back below.
